About

I program in C#, C++ and Python, building games in Unity and Unreal across PC, VR and multiplayer, along with custom editor tools in Unity that make the work around me faster. Microsoft PlayFab and Photon Fusion 2 networking, XR systems, enemy AI systems, procedural generation, scalable structure, I've built all of it by knowing when to lean on a package or API and when to write a system myself, so the pieces work as one, because I genuinely love making games, not just shipping them.

I'm comfortable with clients, good at turning feedback into action, and I keep teams moving without the friction that slows things down.
