Han Zhang

My strongest ground is programming. I work primarily in C# and C++, building games in Unity and Unreal across PC, VR and multiplayer. I've built netcode, XR interaction systems, combat frameworks, AI behaviors, procedural generation, and physics-driven mechanics from scratch. If it runs in a game, I can build it.

On the design side, I focus on level design. I care about how space shapes player behavior, how a layout tells a player where to go without ever saying it out loud, and how small adjustments in geometry can completely change the feel of an encounter.

I've worked in teams where communication matters as much as code. I'm comfortable talking to clients, translating feedback into action, and keeping a team on the same page without the friction that slows projects down.
